# GroundingMode.md

## Purpose

Grounding Mode is a temporary operating framework used when normal life systems have become unstable and a simplified, sustainable structure is required.

The purpose of Grounding Mode is not improvement, optimization, or personal transformation. The purpose is to maintain forward movement until conditions improve and higher-order systems become available again.

Grounding Mode is intended to be engaged after an initial crisis has passed and immediate stabilization has been achieved.

---

## Guiding Principle

> The purpose of Grounding Mode is not improvement, but maintaining forward movement until conditions improve.

When higher-order systems become unavailable, return to proven fundamentals.

Reduce complexity. Increase consistency.

---

## Activation Criteria

Grounding Mode may be activated when three or more Core Life Systems have remained unstable for seven or more consecutive days.

### Core Life Systems

* Physical Health
* Mental Health
* Spiritual Health
* Housing
* Income
* Relationships
* Household Operations
* Transportation
* Administrative Responsibilities
* Community Participation

---

## Relationship to Other Phases

| Phase     | Purpose                               |
| --------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Crisis    | Immediate stabilization and safety    |
| Grounding | Re-establish basic operating rhythm   |
| Recovery  | Rebuild capability and capacity       |
| Expansion | Pursue larger goals and opportunities |

Grounding Mode is not Crisis Mode.

Grounding Mode begins after immediate threats and urgent concerns have been addressed.

---

## Daily Completion Standard

### Progressive Day

A day is considered Progressive when all core Grounding Mode activities are completed:

* Nutrition
* Movement
* Reading
* Writing
* Arithmetic
* Rest

### Challenging Day

A day is considered Challenging when one or more core Grounding Mode activities are not completed.

Challenging days are expected.

The objective is consistency, not perfection.

# Core Components

## Nutrition

### Purpose

The purpose of the nutrition framework is nutritional adequacy with minimal decision-making.

The objective is to reduce complexity while maintaining sufficient energy and nutrient intake.

### Eating Pattern

* Intermittent fasting
* Six to eight hour eating window
* Simple food preparation
* Minimize highly processed foods

### Foundation Foods

* Eggs
* Feta
* Lentils
* Sardines
* Extra Virgin Olive Oil
* Greek Yogurt
* Walnuts
* Bone Broth

### Expansion Foods

* Goat Cheese
* Cheddar Cheese
* Pinto Beans
* Brown Rice
* Salmon
* Chicken Thighs
* Olives
* Pickled Beets
* Sweet Potatoes
* Russet Potatoes
* Cabbage
* Rolled Oats
* Peanut Butter

### Notes

* Weight loss may occur but is not a goal.
* Budget reduction may occur but is not a goal.
* Calories are not tracked.
* Portions are not prescribed.
* Food decisions should remain simple and repeatable.

---

## Movement

### Purpose

Movement exists to maintain physical capability and preserve momentum.

Progress begins at the current level of ability and expands gradually.

### Daily Framework

1. Stretch
2. Walk
3. Stretch

### Walking

Begin with whatever distance or duration is achievable.

Increase duration, distance, or frequency as circumstances allow.

### Foundational Practice

* Bodyweight mobility work
* Headstands
* Backbends
* Handstands

These practices are considered part of the Grounding Mode framework for this individual.

### Rest Days

* One rest day per week

### Progression

#### Tier 1

* Mobility
* Stretching

#### Tier 2

* Walking
* Outdoor exposure

#### Tier 3

* Foundational bodyweight skills
* Progressive challenges

## Reading

### Purpose

Reading supports:

* Spiritual grounding
* Narrative engagement
* Reading comprehension
* Reflection

### Practice

* Scripture reading
* Daily reading guided by Our Daily Bread
* Presence over absence

No minimum page requirement exists.

The objective is consistent engagement.

---

## Writing

### Purpose

Writing supports:

* Journaling
* Structured reflection
* Goal review
* Emotional processing
* Life planning
* Personal documentation

### Minimum Standard

* One page daily

### Suggested Structure

#### Current Circumstances

What is occurring right now?

#### Gratitudes

What remains good, useful, or available?

#### Challenges

What difficulties are present?

#### Planned Actions

What actions will be taken next?

#### Observations

What has been learned or noticed?

* Resource inventory

### Level 2

* Percentages
* Ratios
* Financial projections

### Level 3

* Word problems

### Example Topics

* Available resources
* Resource consumption

---

## Rest

### Purpose

Rest is intentional recovery, not passive consumption.

### Practice

* Rest in a clean and made bed
* Quiet wakeful recovery
* Intentional decompression
* Reduced stimulation when needed

Rest does not require sleep.

---

## Environmental Stability

### Purpose

Environmental stability reduces friction and preserves functionality.

### Practices

#### Bed

* Make bed as early as practical

#### Dishes

* Wash dishes as they are used

#### Laundry

* Reduce washer and dryer usage where practical
* Hang clothing when appropriate
* Refresh garments as needed

#### Trash

* Remove when full

#### Living Space

* Clean as you go
* Return items after use
* Maintain a functional environment

The objective is usability, not perfection.

---

## Outdoor Exposure

### Purpose

Outdoor exposure supports movement, observation, and environmental engagement.

### Standard

Minimum:

* One hour daily

Preferred:

* One to three hours daily

* Contribute

---

# Completion Criteria

Grounding Mode concludes when:

* Core Life Systems have stabilized
* Daily routines have been completed consistently for twenty-one consecutive days

And:

* A sustainable income path has been identified
* Income is being generated
* Income is producing sufficient revenue
* Daily functioning has stabilized

At that point, transition to a long-term Recovery or Expansion framework.

---

# Notes

Grounding Mode is a framework, not a rule set.

The framework should remain simple enough to use during difficult periods and flexible enough to adapt to changing circumstances.

If the framework becomes difficult to maintain, reduce complexity rather than abandon the system entirely.
